## Verdict: (b) Partly done

- This search found no verified paper that formally tests whether USD/JPY's sensitivity to JGB 2Y changed across the 2022-2024 YCC dismantling. The academic samples stop at Dec 2022 (Masujima & Sato) or Dec 2023 (the BOJ and IMF working papers), before the March 2024 exit and the hikes that followed.
- The novelty margin is narrower than assumed. IMF staff (CR 26/75) already document that the yen has decoupled from the US-Japan yield differential since mid-2025 (the 10Y differential narrowed by over 100bp while the yen weakened; their rate driver combines the 2Y and 10Y spreads). Lazard (verified) and JP Morgan (not verified) say the link weakened after 2024. Ferrari et al. predict FX sensitivity is strongest near the lower bound. Every source we found points to a weaker link, not a stronger one, which is the opposite of our current hypothesis.
- What is still new in our version: (1) a formal break test, with break dates set by the data, on daily data from 2016 to 2026; (2) the JP 2Y leg kept separate from the US 2Y leg (Kano & Wada put the 2013 shift on the US side; the IMF combines the 2Y and 10Y spreads in one rate driver and Lazard gives no method, so neither separates the JP 2Y leg); (3) the 2022-2024 transition and the mid-2025 decoupling covered in one framework.
- For Steven to decide: make the test two-sided (rise or fall), and reword the question from "did it rise" to "did it change, and when". This changes the question wording in the plan and on Notion.
